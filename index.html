About the Chapter
SCSN ADUSTECH Wudil Chapter supports chemistry students with an accessible academic resource hub and chapter information.
President: Sen. Ahmad Muhammad Jaji (Sen. Jaji)
University: Aliko Dangote University of Science and Technology, Wudil
Motto: Chemistry reshape the nature
DEPARTMENT HISTORY
History of the Department of Chemistry
The Department of Chemistry forms part of the science-based academic development of Aliko Dangote University of Science and Technology, Wudil. Its story is connected to the establishment and growth of the university.
1988–1991
Early Planning and Establishment
The university’s establishment process dates back to 1988, when the Kano State Government constituted a task force chaired by Professor Ibrahim H. Umar to consider a state-owned university focused on science and technology. A technical committee was subsequently established to examine the recommendations, and an implementation committee followed in 1991.
April 2001
Commencement of Academic Activities
After administrative and financial delays, the university commenced academic activities in April 2001. Its initial academic structure included the Faculty of Agriculture and Agricultural Technology and the Faculty of Science and Science Education (FASSE), within which core scientific disciplines, including Chemistry, contributed to its academic mission.
2005–2007
Accreditation and Academic Development
The information supplied for this history records interim accreditation for the Chemistry programme in 2005 and full accreditation in 2007 by the National Universities Commission (NUC). These milestones represent important stages in the programme’s academic development.
2022
Institutional Renaming
In 2022, Kano University of Science and Technology (KUST), Wudil, was renamed Aliko Dangote University of Science and Technology (ADUSTECH), Wudil, by the Kano State Government. The institution continues to focus on science, technology, innovation and applied learning.
Academic Growth and Development
Academic Programmes
The information provided identifies B.Sc. Chemistry and B.Sc. (Ed.) Chemistry among the Chemistry-related programmes. Current official programme titles and availability should be confirmed against the department’s approved curriculum.
Academic Staff and Leadership
The names supplied for this historical account include Professor Ibrahim U. Kutama and Dr. Ibrahim Isah Fagge. Their affiliations, academic titles and specific contributions should be confirmed by the Department before this profile is treated as a complete official record.
Student Achievements
The information supplied highlights reported first-class graduates in Chemistry and Chemistry Education, including Usman Idris Miga and Izilmasi Muhammad Ya’u. These names and distinctions can be updated or confirmed using official departmental records.
Innovation and Research
The account provided refers to water-powered cooking-stove innovations associated with Chemistry graduates and presentations at national science exhibitions. Further documentation would help identify the innovators, describe the technology accurately and record the relevant exhibitions.
Continuing Legacy
The Department of Chemistry is part of ADUSTECH Wudil’s broader commitment to science education, research and technological development. Its continuing mission includes building scientific knowledge, practical skills and opportunities for students to contribute to society.
